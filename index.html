About

Hands-on social media, built from real communities.

I started managing social media when I founded Biotech Squad in February 2025, a student-led community that now has over 2000 followers on LinkedIn and more than 1000 members in its WhatsApp community. There was no agency or brand team behind it. I planned the content, wrote the posts and answered the messages myself.

In July 2025 I co-founded BioSkillz, a skills-training initiative, and took on its marketing as well. That means two brands, two audiences and two sets of deadlines, which taught me to stay organised and keep each brand sounding like itself.

I also work as a science teacher, so I am used to explaining ideas simply and adjusting my tone for different audiences. That carries straight into writing captions and campaign copy.

Content Workflow

How a campaign goes from idea to post.

  1. Goal and audience

    Decide what the campaign should achieve, such as registrations, awareness or engagement, and who it is for.

  2. Content calendar

    Place posts on a calendar around key dates like launches and deadlines.

  3. Copy and design

    Write the captions and design the visuals in Canva, working with the team on details.

  4. Posting

    Publish on Instagram, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, adjusting the format for each platform.

  5. Community replies

    Answer comments and messages quickly so interested people do not drop off.

  6. Review

    Check engagement and registrations, then use what worked in the next campaign.
